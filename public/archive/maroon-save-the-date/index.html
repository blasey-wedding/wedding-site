Our story

How we got here

  1. 2019

    2019: We met at a friend’s terrible karaoke night

    Casey sang one song badly on purpose. Blake sang three badly by accident. We closed the place down arguing about which was worse.

  2. 2021

    2021: We adopted a dog with opinions

    Biscuit chose us at the shelter, then chose the couch, then chose every other thing we owned.

  3. 2025

    2025: The question

    A sunrise hike, one very nervous proposal, and a ring that almost went over the edge. The answer was yes, eventually, between laughing fits.
